# Threat Scale and Growth of AI-Generated Fake Video/Image Misinformation (Deepfakes), 2024–2026

Research date: 2026-10-18. Several primary-source domains (resemble.ai, weforum.org) could not be fetched directly from this environment (DNS failure), so some figures below rely on search-result snippets of primary pages or on secondary coverage. These are marked. Vendor figures (Resemble AI, Sumsub, Surfshark, GetReal, Hive) come from companies that sell detection or verification products. Treat them as indicative, not neutral.

## 1. Latest quantitative measures of deepfake incidence and harm (2024, 2025, 2026 so far)

### Takeaway
Every available tracker shows a steep rise in documented incidents and losses from 2024 to 2025. Resemble AI counts about 1,567 verified incidents and about $1.28B in documented losses for 2025. Surfshark counts about 580 incidents in H1 2025 against about 150 in all of 2024. Early 2026 brought the largest single non-consensual imagery event on record (Grok on X, millions of sexualized images in about 11 days). All of these numbers are documented-case floors, not estimates of total prevalence.

### Cited Findings
**Incident counts and fraud losses (vendor trackers)**
- Resemble AI Q2 2025 report (published July 16, 2025): 487 verified deepfake incidents in Q2 2025 and about $347M in losses. 84% of incidents targeted women. One case: a French woman lost $850,000 over 18 months to a fake "Brad Pitt." — [Resemble AI Q2 2025 report](https://www.resemble.ai/q2-2025-ai-deepfake-security-report/) (vendor; figures from search snippet, page not directly fetched)
- Resemble AI full-year 2025 report (dated March 20, 2026): 1,567 unique verified incidents in 2025 and about $1.28B in documented fraud losses. More than 80% of incidents disclosed no financial damage, so true losses are likely higher. Corporate fraud: 41 documented incidents, $74.9M in losses, largest single incident $35M. — [Resemble AI 2025 Deepfake Threat Report](https://resemble.ai/2025-deepfake-threat-report) (vendor; snippet only. Methodology differs from the quarterly report, so the two should not be summed or compared directly)
- Surfshark, combining AI Incident Database and Resemble AI data: about 580 deepfake incidents in H1 2025, almost 4x the roughly 150 in all of 2024. Also cites a deepfake investment scam with $35M in losses across more than 6,000 victims. — [Surfshark deepfake fraud losses](https://surfshark.com/research/chart/deepfake-fraud-losses); [Surfshark deepfake social media fraud, Jan 27 2026](https://surfshark.com/research/chart/deepfake-social-media-fraud) (vendor; secondary aggregation)

**Identity-verification fraud (vendor)**
- Sumsub Identity Fraud Report 2025–2026: "multi-step, AI-driven" (sophisticated) fraud rose from 10% of identity fraud in 2024 to 28% in 2025, a 180% increase. The overall fraud rate fell slightly, from 2.6% to 2.2%. In other words, volume is stabilizing while sophistication rises. — [Fintech News SG summary](https://fintechnews.sg/sumsub-identity-fraud-report-2025-2026/) (secondary coverage of a vendor report; original not reached)
- Regional deepfake-attempt growth in the same Sumsub data:
  - APAC: Singapore +158% YoY even as overall fraud fell 12%; Malaysia +408%; Maldives +2100%; synthetic personal data in APAC +142% YoY. — [AAP/Cision Sumsub release, Nov 25 2025](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20251125ae32757)
  - Africa: Zambia +967%, DRC +367%. — [BusinessDay NG](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/africa-enters-high-skill-fraud-era-as-deepfakes-surge-967-in-zambia-367-in-drc/)
  - These are percentage changes on undisclosed bases. Small markets inflate them.
- AI-generated documents went from effectively 0% to about 2% of detected fake documents during 2025 (Sumsub, via secondary compilation). — [Accredify compilation](https://accredify.io/blog/deepfake-and-ai-document-fraud-statistics) (secondary; unverified against original)

**Benchmark corporate fraud case (older, 2024)**
- Arup (UK engineering firm) lost HK$200M (about US$25.6M) across 15 transfers after a Hong Kong finance employee joined a video call in which the "CFO" and colleagues were all deepfakes. Arup confirmed this in May 2024 and reported it to Hong Kong police in January 2024. — [CNN, May 16 2024](https://edition.cnn.com/2024/05/16/tech/arup-deepfake-scam-loss-hong-kong-intl-hnk)

**Non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), 2026**
- The Center for Countering Digital Hate (CCDH) sampled 20,000 of 4.6M images generated by xAI's Grok on X between Dec 29, 2025 and Jan 9, 2026. It estimated about 3M sexualized images in that window, including about 23,338 sexualized images depicting children and nearly 10,000 sexualized cartoons of children. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/355543/grok-generated-23k-sexualized-images-children-watchdog-reports); [Holyrood](https://www.holyrood.com/news/view,grok-generated-around-three-million-sexualised-images-this-month-according-to-research). One secondary source cites about 300,000 instead; the figures conflict.
- US House Energy & Commerce Democrats cited an estimate of at least 1.8M posts of sexualized images of women over the same period and opened an inquiry. — [House E&C Democrats](https://democrats-energycommerce.house.gov/media/press-releases/ec-democrats-investigate-elon-musks-grok-spreading-rampant-non-consensual)
- Regulatory responses:
  - Ofcom (UK) opened a formal Online Safety Act investigation on Jan 12, 2026.
  - Australia, the EU and France opened probes.
  - Indonesia, Malaysia and the Philippines banned Grok.
  - Source for all of the above: [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/355543/grok-generated-23k-sexualized-images-children-watchdog-reports)
- 84% of Q2 2025 incidents in the Resemble tracker targeted women. — [Resemble AI Q2 2025](https://www.resemble.ai/q2-2025-ai-deepfake-security-report/) (vendor)

**News misinformation / public trust**
- Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2025 (YouGov survey of more than 97,000 people in 48 markets): 58% are concerned about telling true from false online, similar to 2024. Concern is 73% in both the US and Africa and 46% in Western Europe. Respondents expect AI-generated news to be cheaper but less trustworthy. — [GIJN summary of DNR 2025](https://gijn.org/stories/2025-reuters-institute-digital-news-report/); [YouGov key findings](https://yougov.com/articles/52379-three-key-findings-from-the-2025-digital-news-report)
- NewsGuard (Oct 17, 2025): Sora 2 produced realistic videos advancing 16 of 20 provably false claims (80%) when prompted. Five of the claims originated from Russian disinformation operations. Each video took minutes and required no expertise. The visible Sora watermark could be removed in under 4 minutes with free online tools. — [NewsGuard special report](https://www.newsguardtech.com/special-reports/openai-sora-seeing-should-not-be-believing/); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/345296/sora-2-fabricate-convincing-deepfakes-on-command-study-finds)

### Inferences
- Documented incidents roughly quadrupled from 2024 to H1 2025, and 2025 documented losses (about $1.28B) are an order of magnitude above Q2 alone. Directionally, this is a pressing and fast-growing problem. However, the trackers count media-reported cases, so part of the growth reflects more reporting and attention.
- The Grok episode shows that the largest harms by volume in 2026 may come from mainstream platform-integrated generators, not fringe tools.
- Fraud is shifting from volume to quality (Sumsub: overall rate down, sophisticated share up), which is the profile expected when generation tools improve.

### Gaps
- No neutral, government-sourced global count of deepfake incidents or losses exists. The FBI IC3 does not break out deepfakes as a category in the sources found. All global counts here come from vendors.
- 2026 year-to-date incident and loss counts from Resemble, Surfshark or similar trackers were not found.
- No quantitative tally of deepfake election incidents for 2024–2026 (e.g., number of incidents per election) was found within the search budget.
- No hard Indian incidence figures were found.

## 2. Capability and accessibility of current video generators; human and detector accuracy

### Takeaway
Frontier generators now produce short clips with synchronized audio that humans identify at roughly chance to about 60% accuracy in most studies. Open-weight models (Wan 2.2, Apache 2.0) put comparable capability on consumer hardware with no safeguards. Off-the-shelf open-source detectors lose about half their AUC on real-world 2024 deepfakes.

### Cited Findings
**Generators**
- Veo 3 (Google) generates video with natively synchronized audio: dialogue, ambient sound, music and effects. — [Novita blog comparison](https://blogs.novita.ai/wan-2-2-vs-veo-3-which-is-the-better-match-for-you/) (vendor blog; the May 2025 launch date was not confirmed by sources found)
- Wan 2.2 was released by Alibaba Tongyi Lab on July 29, 2025 under Apache 2.0, with code and weights supporting text-to-video and image-to-video up to 720p. Sources conflict on whether later versions (e.g., Wan 2.7) are also open. — [Thunder Compute](https://www.thundercompute.com/blog/best-open-source-ai-video-generation-models); [Evolink](https://evolink.ai/blog/wan-2-7-vs-veo-3) (vendor blogs)
- Sora 2 (OpenAI, fall 2025) produced convincing false-claim videos 80% of the time when prompted. Its visible watermark is removable in under 4 minutes, and OpenAI says it also embeds C2PA metadata. — [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/special-reports/openai-sora-seeing-should-not-be-believing/)
- Commentary at launch: "The long-predicted deepfake dystopia has arrived with Sora 2." — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/the-long-predicted-deepfake-dystopia-has-arrived-with-sora-2/) (opinion)

**Human detection**
- A study of 87 participants on Veo 3, LTX Video and Wan VACE clips found 60% average accuracy. Accuracy varied by method: image-to-video 79%, real footage 64%, outpainting 49%, text-to-video 43% (below chance). — [UNIST ScholarWorks](https://scholarworks.unist.ac.kr/handle/201301/90247) (small sample)
- A Maastricht student study (109 respondents, 2025 Veo/Sora clips without audio, convenience sample) found 52.4% accuracy, essentially chance. — [Maastricht EdLab PDF](https://excellence-edlab.maastrichtuniversity.nl/sites/excellenceedlab/files/2026-05/2025-2026%2C%20Media-Steffen%20Kuenn.pdf) (student work; not representative)
- Video Reality Test / VideoASMR-Bench (arXiv 2512.13281, Dec 2025) tested Veo 3.1-Fast and Sora 2. Videos from the best generator (Veo 3.1-Fast) were judged fake only 12.54% of the time. Humans outperformed vision-language models (reported human figures of 81–89% vary between write-ups). — [arXiv 2512.13281](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.13281); [Neurohive summary](https://neurohive.io/en/state-of-the-art/ai-models-are-13-worse-than-humans-at-detecting-generated-asmr-videos/)
- FakeParts (arXiv 2508.21052, 80 participants, older models including Veo 2) found 75.3% average human accuracy. Partial or local manipulations were harder to spot. — [arXiv 2508.21052](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.21052)

**Automated detection**
- Deepfake-Eval-2024 (arXiv 2503.02857, March 2025; University of Washington and TrueMedia.org authors including Oren Etzioni):
  - Dataset: in-the-wild 2024 deepfakes from 88 websites in 52 languages, about 44–45 hours of video.
  - Open-source state-of-the-art detectors lost about 50% of AUC for video, 48% for audio and 45% for images compared with earlier academic benchmarks.
  - Commercial and fine-tuned models did better but still fell short of human forensic analysts.
  - Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02857v1); [HF papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2503.02857); dataset at github.com/nuriachandra/Deepfake-Eval-2024
- Gartner (2024 prediction): by 2026, 30% of enterprises will no longer consider identity verification and authentication tools reliable in isolation because of AI-generated face deepfakes. Current presentation-attack-detection standards don't cover digital injection attacks. Injection attacks rose 200% in 2023. — [FutureCIO](https://futurecio.tech/deepfakes-to-render-identity-verification-and-authentication-solutions-inefficient/); [Silicon.eu](https://silicon.eu/ai-generated-deepfakes-challenge-biometric-authentication-12487.html)

### Inferences
- Human accuracy depends heavily on the task. For pure text-to-video from 2025-generation models it sits at or below chance (43–52%). It is higher for image-to-video or when viewers know they are being tested. Real-world scrolling conditions are likely worse than lab conditions.
- The combination of a large detector generalization gap, removable watermarks and open-weight models argues that post-hoc detection alone cannot carry the load. This supports provenance approaches (C2PA, signing at capture), but C2PA metadata is also strippable.

### Gaps
- No large, representative (probability-sampled) human-detection study for Sora 2 or Veo 3 with audio was found. Most studies are small preprints.
- No published in-the-wild detector benchmark covering 2025–2026 content (a "Deepfake-Eval-2025") was found.
- Kling and Runway capability or misuse data were not found within the search budget.

## 3. The "liar's dividend" and documented cases

### Takeaway
The liar's dividend (Chesney and Citron, 2019) is the benefit liars gain when the public can be persuaded that authentic media is fake. It moved from theory into practice in 2025 when the US President dismissed an authentic, staff-acknowledged video as "probably AI." Courts face the mirror problem: fake evidence submitted as real.

### Cited Findings
- Concept origin: Chesney (UT Austin) and Citron (then BU Law), 2019: "A skeptical public will be primed to doubt the authenticity of real audio and video evidence." — [ISPR/AP coverage](https://ispr.info/2025/09/04/liars-dividend-president-of-united-states-blames-ai-to-avoid-accountability-and-embraces-doing-so/)
- September 2025 White House window video:
  - Asked about viral footage of an object being thrown from a White House window, President Trump said "No, that's probably AI," although his press team had indicated to reporters that the video was real. He then endorsed blaming AI as a tactic.
  - A CNN analyst called it "a scary preview of a future in which deepfakes — and the fears surrounding AI itself — will become tools to deny reality."
  - Sources: [Euronews](https://euronews.com/next/2025/09/04/donald-trump-blames-ai-for-video-showing-items-thrown-from-white-house-is-it-the-new-fake-); [Boston Globe/AP](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/09/04/business/politicians-blaming-artificial-intelligence); [Sonoma News/AP](https://www.sonomanews.com/2025/09/04/trump-blaming-ai)
- Brennan Center analysis of deepfakes, elections and the liar's dividend. — [Brennan Center](https://www.brennancenter.org/index%2Ephp/es/our-work/research-reports/contenidos-ultrafalsos-deepfakes-elecciones-dividendo-mentiroso)

**Courts**
- Mendones v. Cushman & Wakefield (Alameda County Superior Court, California):
  - Judge Victoria Kolakowski dismissed a housing case on Sept 9 (2025, per coverage dates) after finding that at least two exhibits, including witness video "Exhibit 6C," were AI deepfakes.
  - Her suspicion was triggered by a monotone voice, fuzzy features and repeated expressions.
  - The court lacked "the time, funding, or technical expertise" for forensic analysis.
  - Reconsideration was denied on Nov 6. It is reported as one of the first detected deepfake-evidence submissions.
  - Sources: [NBC News](https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/national-international/ai-generated-evidence-showing-up-in-court-alarms-judges/4017477/); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/110317-judge-dismisses-lawsuit-twice-due-alleged-deepfake-video.html); [DDG](https://www.ddg.fr/actualite/when-deepfakes-enter-the-courtroom-a-landmark-california-decision)
- US federal rules: on June 10, 2025 the Advisory Committee approved proposed Federal Rule of Evidence 707 (machine-generated evidence held to expert-reliability standards) for public comment. The vote was 8-1, with DOJ dissenting. A deepfake-specific Rule 901(c) was discussed, but the committee leaned "wait-and-see." — [Cole Schotz](https://www.coleschotz.com/news-and-publications/proposed-federal-rule-of-evidence-707-to-protect-against-ai-created-fake-evidence/); [Debevoise](https://www.debevoise.com/insights/publications/2025/03/federal-judicial-conference-to-revise-rules-of)

### Inferences
- The liar's dividend is now an openly used political tactic at the highest level. It erodes evidentiary value for authentic footage, the harm that provenance-at-capture is best placed to address.
- Courts lack the resources to authenticate media. Each detected case likely implies undetected ones.

### Gaps
- No quantitative measure of how often real footage is falsely dismissed as AI was found.
- Non-US liar's dividend cases (e.g., India, Europe) were not found within the search budget.
- The final status of FRE 707 after public comment was not confirmed.

## 4. Credible forecasts of growth

### Takeaway
Forecasters converge on continued growth. Deloitte projects US gen-AI-enabled fraud rising from $12.3B (2023) to $40B (2027), a 32% CAGR. The WEF ranks misinformation and disinformation among the top short-term global risks for the third straight year. Gartner predicted that face biometrics alone would become unreliable for 30% of enterprises by 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Deloitte Center for Financial Services (2024): gen AI could push US fraud losses from $12.3B in 2023 to $40B by 2027 (32% CAGR). Gen-AI email fraud alone could reach about $11.5B by 2027 in an "aggressive" adoption scenario. This is a projection, not a measurement. — [Deloitte Insights](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/financial-services/deepfake-banking-fraud-risk-on-the-rise.html)
- WEF Global Risks Report 2026 (January 2026): misinformation and disinformation ranks #2 on the 2-year (to 2028) outlook. Cyber insecurity ranks #6. Chapter 1 text reportedly places it #5 (7% of respondents) in the current-year snapshot. Coverage frames AI as climbing the risk ladder "as trust erodes." Sources conflict on the survey size (more than 900 vs more than 1,300 experts).
  - [WEF GRR 2026](https://cn.weforum.org/publications/global-risks-report-2026/) (primary page, not directly fetchable here; snippet only)
  - [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/other-countries/wef-notes-growing-global-risks-associated-with-disinformation)
  - [Statista](https://statista.com/chart/35680/major-global-risks-short-and-long-term-in-2026/)
  - [TimesLive](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-01-24-arthur-goldstuck-ai-climbs-the-wef-risk-ladder-as-trust-erodes/)
  - For context: the WEF ranked misinformation and disinformation the #1 2-year risk in the 2024 and 2025 reports (from background knowledge, not verified in this session).
- Gartner (Feb 2024 prediction): 30% of enterprises will not consider IDV/authentication reliable in isolation by 2026 because of deepfakes. — [FutureCIO](https://futurecio.tech/deepfakes-to-render-identity-verification-and-authentication-solutions-inefficient/)
- Sumsub: the share of sophisticated AI-driven fraud nearly tripled (10% to 28%) from 2024 to 2025. — [Fintech News SG](https://fintechnews.sg/sumsub-identity-fraud-report-2025-2026/) (vendor)

### Inferences
- Measured 2025 data (Resemble documented losses of about $1.28B, Surfshark's 4x incident growth) is consistent with, or ahead of, the 2024 forecasts. Forecasts made before Veo 3 and Sora 2 likely understate video-specific risk.

### Gaps
- The often-repeated "Europol: 90% of online content may be synthetically generated by 2026" claim could NOT be verified to a Europol primary source in this session. Do not use it without verification; it traces to a 2022 Europol Innovation Lab report quoting an outside expert.
- Entrust/Onfido 2025–2026 figures (e.g., a "deepfake attempt every five minutes" in 2024) were not retrieved in this session.
- No India-specific growth forecast was found.

## 5. Notable 2025–2026 incidents of viral fake video/imagery

### Takeaway
2025 showed text-to-video fakes going viral in real conflicts within weeks of Veo 3's release. Sora 2 enabled on-demand fakes of real people and events, and Grok on X produced mass NCII in early 2026. Recycled old footage nonetheless remained the larger share of conflict misinformation.

### Cited Findings
**Israel–Iran war, June 2025**
- AI-generated clips falsely showed destruction in Tel Aviv and at Ben Gurion Airport. AFP traced them to an AI-content TikTok account. — [Arab News/AFP](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2605459/amp); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2025/06/23/digital-deception-misinformation-war-escalates-as-ai-deepfakes-fake-war-footage-flood-social-media-amid-iran-israel-conflict/181281)
- GetReal Security linked "apocalyptic" war-damage videos to Google Veo 3. A Tehran Times post carried a visible Veo watermark. — [Hespress](https://en.hespress.com/113702-ai-generated-videos-fuel-disinformation-amid-iran-israel-conflict-experts-warn.html)
- A fake clip of "Israelis pleading with Iran" bore a Veo watermark and was flagged by SynthID and Hive. — [Factly](https://factly.in/ai-generated-visuals-created-using-googles-veo-tool-are-falsely-shared-as-israelis-pleading-with-iran-to-stop-the-war)
- Hany Farid noted that Veo 3 clips are typically 8 seconds long or chains of 8-second clips. — [Berkeley iSchool](https://www.ischool.berkeley.edu/news/2025/old-war-videos-are-being-recycled-fake-news-hany-farid-explains)
- NewsGuard found 51 websites pushing false claims. DW noted that recycled old footage dominated, with AI a smaller part. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/tech-fuelled-misinformation-distorts-iran-israel-fighting-1.500171436)

**Sora 2, October 2025**
- NewsGuard produced fake videos of a Moldovan election official destroying pro-Russian ballots, a toddler detained by US immigration officers, and a Coca-Cola Super Bowl pullout. Separately, Sora deepfakes of Martin Luther King Jr. and other public figures caused controversy. — [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/special-reports/openai-sora-seeing-should-not-be-believing/); [Marketplace](https://www.marketplace.org/episode/2025/11/04/sora-2s-disinformation-problem)

**Grok on X, Dec 2025–Jan 2026**
- An estimated 3M sexualized images, about 23k of them depicting children, in 11 days, followed by multi-country regulatory action. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/355543/grok-generated-23k-sexualized-images-children-watchdog-reports)

**Celebrity romance fraud**
- The "Brad Pitt" scam cost a French victim $850,000. — [Resemble AI Q2 2025](https://www.resemble.ai/q2-2025-ai-deepfake-security-report/)

**Courts**
- Mendones deepfake witness video (California, 2025), described in Section 3.

**India (regulatory response)**
- In October 2025, MeitY proposed amendments to the IT Rules requiring labelling of "synthetically generated information." The draft required labels covering at least 10% of the visual area or the first 10% of audio duration. Sources conflict on the final status: some say deepfake provisions have been in force since Feb 20, 2026. Verify against the gazette notification. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/10/223-unpacking-draft-it-amendment-rules-synthetic-information/); [S&S Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/2025-it-rules-amendment-regulating-synthetically-generated-information-in-indias-ai-and-privacy-landscape/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/social-media/1721986/indias-new-framework-for-online-content-regulation-an-overview-of-the-recent-amendments-to-the-it-intermediary-guidelines-rules)

### Inferences
- Veo 3's fakes appeared in a live war within about a month of its release, showing how quickly new generator capability converts into real-world misinformation. Visible watermarks helped fact-checkers in 2025, but they are removable (NewsGuard), so they cannot be relied upon.
- India's labelling mandate signals that major jurisdictions treat the problem as pressing enough to regulate at the platform level.

### Gaps
- Specific 2025–2026 election deepfake incidents were not documented within the search budget: for example Germany (Feb 2025), Canada (Apr 2025), Ireland/Romania/Moldova (2025), US 2026 midterm primaries and India state elections.
- Disaster-related fakes (e.g., hurricane or earthquake imagery in 2025) were also not covered.
- India–Pakistan conflict (Operation Sindoor, May 2025) deepfake videos were searched for but not found in results.
- EU-specific data (e.g., the EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations, applicable from August 2026; EDMO counts) was not gathered.
